# Cost of Living Visualiser

Data Visualisation 4 group project – BSc (Hons) Software Design with AI for Cloud Computing, Technological University of the Shannon (TUS).

## About

A country can have a high minimum wage and still be hard to live in once rent, food, utilities and transport are paid for. This project compares income with essential living costs across European countries. The aim is to help people who are thinking of moving abroad for work or study judge how affordable a country is likely to be for them.

**Primary audience:** immigrants, students and others thinking of moving to another country.

## Main Questions

- How does the cost of living compare between European countries?
- Does a higher minimum wage cover higher living costs?
- Which expense categories (rent, food, transport, education, leisure) explain the differences between countries?

## Data Sources

- [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat) – minimum wages, price levels, household expenditure
- [Numbeo](https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/) – prices for rent, groceries, transport, utilities and leisure
- [OECD Data Explorer](https://data-explorer.oecd.org/) – price levels and purchasing power
- [Daft.ie](https://www.daft.ie/report) – Irish rental prices

## Planned Features

- Visualisations comparing wages with living costs, breaking down costs by category and showing the income left after expenses
- **Interactive website:** users will enter their own details, such as expected income, rent or household situation. The visualisation will then adjust to show affordability for their circumstances rather than only national averages.
